The Stories

It starts with a story.

Each theme begins with a Little Dude story that introduces a big idea in a way young children can understand.

The characters give children something concrete to talk about, while giving teachers a natural way to begin the conversation.

Kindness Feelings Friendship Empathy Respect Courage Manners Growth Mindset Brain Vacation
The Lessons

Then we put it into practice.

Every lesson follows the same simple rhythm. Clear guidance gives teachers the structure they need, while leaving plenty of room for conversation, movement, play and the unexpected moments that happen with real children.

Learning through play, practice and doing.

Teachers can use the same language, strategies and routines during play, transitions, friendship struggles and big feelings throughout the day.
