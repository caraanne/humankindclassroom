Why humanKIND Classroom

Social skills aren't learned in one lesson. They're practised every day.

Children learn kindness, friendship, empathy and respect through stories, play, relationships and everyday experiences.

That's why humanKIND Classroom is designed to become part of your school culture, not just another visiting enrichment program.

We give educators practical tools, engaging stories and a consistent teaching framework they can use throughout the school day.

Meet Cara

Created by an educator, for educators.

I'm Cara Zelas, an early childhood educator and creator of humanKIND Classroom and the Big World of Little Dude.

After years of working with young children, I've seen how much difference it makes when social skills are taught through stories, play and everyday experiences.

I created humanKIND Classroom to give teachers practical, engaging resources that make these important skills easier to teach.

My goal is simple: help educators create classrooms where kindness, friendship, empathy and belonging are part of everyday life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions, answered.

What ages is humanKIND Classroom designed for?

The curriculum is designed for children ages 4–8, from Pre-K through Second Grade.

Do our teachers need additional training?

No prior training in social skills teaching is required. Both partnerships include a 90-minute professional development workshop, teaching resources and practical implementation guidance.

Do teachers deliver the lessons in the Guided Partnership?

Yes! That's the idea. Cara demonstrates the approach in a live classroom lesson and trains your teaching team to lead the curriculum themselves. This helps educators incorporate the strategies into daily routines and reinforce the learning beyond individual lessons.

Can we implement the curriculum at our own pace?

Yes. The nine themes can be incorporated into your existing classroom schedule and routines.

What if we have more than two classrooms?

We can create a custom quote for schools with three or more participating classrooms. Email Cara to discuss your school's needs.

Is there a more affordable option for individual classrooms?

Yes! Our $397 Classroom Kit includes the complete curriculum, nine books, Little Dude & Bird plush toys, printed handbook, digital teaching resources and a 30-minute Professional Development online workshop. Explore the Classroom Kit
