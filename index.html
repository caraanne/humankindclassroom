The humanKIND Curriculum

A curriculum your whole school will feel good about.

Built on the Big World of Little Dude book series — each unit is anchored in a story, so lessons feel like storytime, not a program. Because that's exactly what four-year-olds need.

  • ✓ Story-based learning — every unit begins with a Little Dude book, making social skills feel natural and memorable.
  • ✓ Zero-prep options — open it, read it, teach it. No planning required beyond the five minutes before circle time.
  • ✓ Builds real skills — social skills, executive functioning, communication, and classroom connection. Not just feelings charts.
  • ✓ Montessori-inspired — hands-on, child-led, and grounded in how young children actually develop.
  • ✓ Discussion guides included — teachers have everything they need to lead real conversations about kindness, friendship, and courage.
