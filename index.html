The Classroom Challenge

Teachers shouldn't have to repeat the same directions all day.

Waiting. Listening. Joining play. Giving others space. Managing big feelings. These skills shape the entire classroom day, but teachers are often expected to address them in the moment without dedicated time, shared language or practical tools.

humanKIND Classroom turns everyday challenges into simple opportunities to teach and practise the skills children need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions, answered.

Is the digital program really free?

Yes. The digital humanKIND Classroom curriculum, nine digital books, classroom visuals and how-to video are free for individual classroom use.

What ages is the program designed for?

humanKIND Classroom is designed for children ages 4 to 7, including Pre-K, Kindergarten and Grade 1.

Do I need to teach the themes in order?

No. Educators can follow the suggested sequence or begin with the themes that respond to their classroom's current needs.

How much preparation is required?

The lessons and routines are designed to be practical and low-preparation. Educators can use individual activities or follow the complete program.

Can my whole school use the free program?

Individual educators can access the free program for their own classrooms. Schools wanting coordinated implementation and direct support can explore a school partnership.

Do you offer virtual and in-person support?

Yes. Cara offers virtual and in-person humanKIND Classroom Labs, demonstration lessons, coaching and customized school partnerships.

Is shipping included in the Classroom Kit?

U.S. shipping is included. International shipping is calculated separately.
